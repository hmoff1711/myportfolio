03 / From the real world

Five case notes.

Real infrastructure work, presented without production identifiers. Each note records the intervention, the evidence and what remained open.

01Who owns the file?Dell EMC Isilon · OneFS 9.x · SMB · ACLs · Protocol Audit

Create without destroying.

The access model called for read, copy and create access in protected media folders, without modify, delete, rename or permission changes. Other folders needed to remain fully writable.

The work

  • Inspected recursive ownership and ACL data with isi get -DD.
  • Correlated SMB activity using isi_audit_viewer protocol-audit records.
  • Normalized owner, group and inherited ACLs through OneFS PermissionRepair.

What was verified

Existing objects and tested restricted, full-access and administrative paths matched the intended access model.

Open boundary · native ownership

Newly created files continued through the documented owner-repair control while native ownership behavior remained under evaluation. This case does not claim that native ownership was solved.

02Same service. Different speed.Ubuntu 24.04 · ProFTPD / mod_sftp · iperf3 · RDP

A symptom that followed the clock.

Daytime SFTP throughput fell to roughly 1.5–3 Mbit/s but reached 90–97 Mbit/s in the evening. The task was to distinguish service configuration from network-path behavior.

The work

  • Validated host keys, chroot boundaries, setgid directories, permissions and Umask 007.
  • Disabled the OpenSSH SFTP subsystem so ProFTPD handled SFTP exclusively.
  • Compared transfer ports, time windows, iperf3 throughput and RDP behavior.

What was verified

Authentication, uploads and chroot isolation passed validation. Matching daytime degradation in iperf3 and RDP moved the next investigation step to the network path, QoS and provider track.

Open boundary · throughput

The reported speeds describe different time windows. They are not an improvement attributed to the hardening work. The throughput root cause was not closed.

03Knowing when to stop.Remote Access Plus · Active Directory · HTTPS/TLS · GPO · SYSVOL

Secure the management path first.

Prepare a secure Remote Access Plus management path and verify the controls required before expanding an endpoint-agent rollout beyond the pilot computer.

The work

  • Moved web administration to HTTPS 443 and closed the previous management port.
  • Verified certificate trust from a domain workstation.
  • Confirmed startup-GPO targeting and package availability in SYSVOL.

What was verified

HTTPS, certificate trust and pilot GPO controls passed validation. Wider deployment stayed behind an intentional quality gate until the vendor setup.bat met unattended-install requirements.

Open boundary · wider rollout

The wider rollout remained on hold behind the unattended-install quality gate. Secure management and pilot controls were verified; a completed deployment is not claimed.

04The account beneath the session.Windows · SMB · Credential Manager · OneFS

Which identity is the client using?

An SMB access incident needed to be separated from OneFS access controls. The useful starting point was the identity in the active SMB connection.

The work

Get-SmbConnection exposed the account actually used by the SMB client. The intervention was limited to the matching cmdkey target in the saved credential state.

What was verified

Removing only the matching saved credential restored the credential prompt and separated the incident from OneFS access controls.

Scope of change

One matching credential target. The observed result was a restored prompt; the incident was isolated from storage ACL behavior.

05Before mail, a name.HP LaserJet Enterprise MFP M528 · DNS · Microsoft 365 · SMTP

Begin with the identical device.

A scan-to-email incident on an HP LaserJet Enterprise MFP M528 was investigated by comparing two identical devices before attributing the issue to Microsoft 365 authentication.

The work

The affected device had no DNS server configured. The comparison identified hostname resolution as the fault boundary to address.

What was verified

Hostname resolution was restored. Mail delivery returned to its separate validation step without misattributing the issue to Microsoft 365 authentication.

Open boundary · mail delivery

A DNS recovery is not evidence of completed mail delivery. SMTP and delivery require their own validation.
